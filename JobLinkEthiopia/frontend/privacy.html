At JobLink Ethiopia, we take your trust and privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how JobLink Ethiopia collects, utilizes, stores, and safeguards personal information provided by job seekers, employers, recruiters, and website visitors across our digital platform and services.
1

Introduction & Scope

This policy applies to all users interacting with the JobLink Ethiopia website (joblink-ethiopia.onrender.com), applicant tracking tools, employer management dashboards, and communication channels.

By creating an account or using JobLink Ethiopia, you consent to the data practices described in this document. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please refrain from submitting personal records or using our services.

2

Information We Collect

We collect personal and business information necessary to facilitate employment matching and maintain system security:

  • Job Seeker Information: Full legal name, email address, telephone contact, physical address or region in Ethiopia, profile avatar, curriculum vitae (CV/resume), academic qualifications, professional history, skills list, portfolio website, GitHub profile, and LinkedIn URL.
  • Employer & Recruiter Information: Company name, registered business address, employer representative name, business email, official phone number, company logo, industry sector, corporate website, and verification credentials.
  • Job Application Details: Cover letters, custom questionnaire responses, application submission timestamps, and hiring status records.
  • Financial & Transactional Records: Transaction IDs, payment amounts, timestamp data, and payment channels (such as Telebirr, CBE Birr, or Chapa). Note: JobLink Ethiopia never stores complete credit card numbers or bank account PINs.
  • Technical & Usage Telemetry: IP addresses, browser specifications, operating system details, device characteristics, referrer headers, and navigation access logs.
3

How We Use Your Information

JobLink Ethiopia processes your data for specific, lawful purposes:

  • Connecting Candidates with Vacancies: Enabling job seekers to apply directly to vacancies, build and maintain digital resumes, and receive customized job recommendation alerts;
  • Employer Recruitment Operations: Allowing verified employers to review applicant submissions, manage candidate pipelines, and communicate interview invitations;
  • Account Verification & Security: Enforcing Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication (2FA/TOTP) for administrators, IP whitelisting, and detecting unauthorized intrusion attempts;
  • Notifications & Communications: Transmitting job match alerts, application status updates, system security notifications, and critical operational notices;
  • Platform Performance & Research: Analyzing aggregated employment trends and improving interface accessibility for Ethiopian youth.
4

Information Sharing & Disclosure

JobLink Ethiopia does NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies. We disclose data solely in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Employers You Apply To: When you submit an application through JobLink Ethiopia, your resume, contact details, and application materials are made accessible to the prospective employer.
  • Trusted Infrastructure Partners: We engage verified cloud hosting, database hosting, and email transmission providers (e.g., Aiven Cloud MySQL, Render, Nodemailer) bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Payment Processors: For employer subscription or featured job fees, transactional records are processed securely through certified payment gateways including Chapa, Telebirr, and Commercial Bank of Ethiopia.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose records if required by Ethiopian statutory mandates, court subpoenas, or law enforcement orders to prevent fraudulent conduct or protect public safety.
5

Data Security Standards

We employ robust organizational and technical safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized access, data alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Bcrypt Password Hashing: Passwords are never stored in plain text and are salted and hashed using modern bcrypt cryptographic standards;
  • Encrypted Transport: All data transmitted between your browser and JobLink Ethiopia servers is protected using Secure Sockets Layer / Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS);
  • Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Server-side middleware verifies user roles on every privileged request, preventing unauthorized cross-role dashboard access;
  • Administrative IP Whitelisting & 2FA: Sensitive administrative controls and system metrics are restricted to whitelisted IP addresses and protected by two-factor authentication;
  • Anti-FOUC & Session Shields: Dashboards employ immediate authentication verifications to protect cached user documents from unauthorized local viewing.
6

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account profile, job applications, and CV documents for as long as your account remains active or as needed to deliver our services.

If you choose to delete your account, your profile data, CV, and active job alerts will be permanently erased or anonymized from active operational databases within thirty (30) days, except where retention of specific records is required by Ethiopian tax, accounting, or audit laws.

7

Your Privacy Rights & Choices

As a JobLink Ethiopia user, you have full control over your personal data:

  • Review & Update: You may edit your personal details, work history, skills, contact numbers, and CV at any time via your Seeker or Employer dashboard;
  • Alert Preferences: You may modify or delete automated job alert subscriptions anytime via the "Job Alerts" tab;
  • Account Closure & Data Erasure: You may request full account deletion by submitting a verified request to support@joblinkethiopia.com;
  • Data Portability: You may download a copy of your stored CV and application history directly from your dashboard.
8

Cookies & Local Storage

JobLink Ethiopia utilizes local browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) and essential session cookies to deliver core functionality:

  • Authentication Tokens: Secure JWT tokens are stored to maintain your authenticated session as you navigate dashboard features;
  • Theme Preferences: Your dark mode or light mode visual preference is saved locally to provide seamless rendering across page loads;
  • Session State: Transient form values and search filters are cached temporarily to enhance user experience.

We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies or third-party behavioral advertising pixels.

9

Third-Party Integrations & External Links

JobLink Ethiopia may contain links to external corporate websites, LinkedIn profiles, GitHub repositories, or external applicant tracking systems specified by employers.

Please note that once you leave our Platform, your data is governed by the privacy practices of the destination third party. We encourage you to review their respective privacy statements before providing personal records.

10

Children's Privacy

Our services are directed to individuals of legal working age (minimum 18 years of age or eligible working age under Ethiopian law). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal records from individuals under the age of 18. If we discover that personal details of a minor have been collected without parental consent, we will promptly purge the data from our servers.

11

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological advancements, legal developments, or operational updates. When revisions occur, the updated policy will be published here with a renewed "Last Updated" timestamp. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
