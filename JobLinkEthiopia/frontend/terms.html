Employer Obligations & Verification

Employers and recruiters utilizing JobLink Ethiopia agree to conduct recruitment operations ethically, transparently, and in strict compliance with Ethiopian Labor Proclamations:

  • Zero Recruitment Fees: In accordance with Ethiopian labor regulations and international best practices, employers are strictly forbidden from charging applicants any application fee, registration charge, training deposit, or interview processing cost.

Job Postings & Content Standards

All job opportunities published on JobLink Ethiopia are subject to automated validation and administrative moderation. We strictly prohibit the following types of job listings:

  • Multi-Level Marketing (MLM), pyramid schemes, or commission-only recruitment solicitations;
  • Listings requiring advance monetary deposits, equipment purchasing from unverified third parties, or check-cashing schemes;
  • Postings containing misleading salary descriptions, undisclosed workplace locations, or vague responsibilities;
  • Positions that violate public policy, labor dignity, or Ethiopian commercial statutes.
Moderation Policy: JobLink Ethiopia reserves the right to unpublish, edit, or permanently remove any job listing that fails our quality benchmarks, generates validated user reports, or violates anti-fraud guidelines without liability.
